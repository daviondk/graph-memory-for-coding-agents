# Selective detail disclosure after retrieval
Locally registered before making assignments and examining individual choices, correctness, tokens, or relation rows in this study. Previous public-in-project aggregates are known (75/99 full, 74/99 short, four changed decisions). This is a secondary policy replay on completed, fixed potential responses. The 99 are a development benchmark, not fresh independent data.

**Research question.** Given the same five trajectories returned by the locked retrieval stage, can a one-call agent identify in advance when edit details contain distinctive evidence that the short cards do not already associate with a unique candidate?

**Policy at write time.** Form a per-record lexical signature from the first 40 whitespace-delimited words of observed edit evidence (the exact cap of the original full reader). Tokenize ASCII identifiers by the pattern [A-Za-z_][A-Za-z_0-9]*; casefold, require at least four characters and at least one letter, and ignore the literal language/path stoplist in policy.py. Keep terms found in at most 15% of the complete 300-card library. The compact postings store trace keys, not raw edit text. Record exact index bytes and count. No target repository index, human label, model output or grade is used.
 
**Read decision.** Process the issue plus the five already selected short cards exactly as truncated in the original reader (175 words of symptom, 90 words of closure). Open the 40-word edit field on all five cards iff an issue token belongs to precisely one of the five edit signatures and that token does not already occur in exactly that same singleton among the five short cards. Otherwise hide edits on all five. Case-insensitive exact token matching only; source order is independent of grades. No gate prompt, no second model call. All other content, model, selection slate, presentation order and decode choices equal the logged experiments. Expose the full field exactly as in the original full reader whenever the rule fires. Under the frozen inputs the decision can be made for one issue online without using other incoming problems. No tuning on observed allocation or outcomes.

**Controls.** Never, always, and seeded equal-frequency random disclosure. The latter selects precisely k of 99 rows by ascending SHA-256('read-policy-null-v1|' + ordinal), with k fixed by the gate's input-only assignments. This is a reproducible batch comparator. Describe also the full distribution over all uniformly drawn size-k subsets, not just one random seed. Obtain exact Match@1 distribution by multivariate hypergeometric counting of full-only correct, short-only correct and other outcomes. Compare gate to uniform allocation with the upper-tail inclusion probability P(random Match >= gate Match); this assesses targeting on this fixed collection. Calculate exact expected tokens and an empirical central 95% interval by 20,000 uniform allocations, seed 20261116. Equal rate need not imply equal token use.

**Outcomes and population.** All 99 frozen Qwen3.8-27B full and matched no_edits responses. A policy selects the observed answer and observed prompt/completion token usage from just one of these. New LLM calls = 0, calls represented per deployed question = 1. Compute Match@1 with all valid release relations, plus first / last per source-order sensitivity. Report new successes, lost successes, changed decisions, error rate amongst triggered rows, input and output tokens and cost differences vs never/always/random. A perfect unachievable selector choosing between the two observed outputs is a descriptive ceiling on these 99, not a baseline. Show nondominated points (maximize correct, minimize prompt tokens) only among the four tested policies. Count all queries even if their labelled experience lies outside a single-label pool. Falsification: no enrichment of helpful full reads relative to uniform disclosure, or failure to retain observed full-card accuracy, weakens the rationale for this specific trigger.

**Uncertainty.** Resample whole repositories (99 questions, 12 represented repos), 10,000 replicates with seed 20261116, percentile interval for per-query differences in Match@1 and input-token usage. Fixed benchmark and dependent repositories restrict generalization; only a few discordant potential outcomes place a hard limit on evidence. Also compute leave-one-repository-out influence of the observed point estimate without fitting. Paired exact McNemar is descriptive for baseline contrasts. No multiple model selection, subgroup tuning, learned policy, equivalence claim, or projection to patch-solving performance. No independent reruns; use relies on replay of logged full vs short requests with identical settings.

**Lock order.** Write protocol and policy/checksum; compute assignments using ONLY queries.json, cards.json and blind_ranks.json, hash and timestamp. Then open existing response logs and gold in a separate evaluator. Log every policy assignment and per-instance selected outcome, exact source hashes, aggregate scores, costs and runtime. Package both new artifacts and documented parent archive requirement.
