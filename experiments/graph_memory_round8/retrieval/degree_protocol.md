# Independent replication of a degree-aware graph policy

This is a prospectively frozen follow-up informed by two discordant outcomes on the earlier pool-generation panel. In that panel a one-hop query promoted 6 candidate neighbors in a gain and 298 in a loss; this two-case observation generated the hypothesis. No outcome from the new panel was accessed for design. 

Take the next four eligible issues per repository by the original seed after excluding all 36 previously sampled SWE-bench Lite IDs. Restrict to the same complete official function corpora, three repositories, filter markers and read-only static task. If fewer than four eligible tasks remain in a repo, include all eligible remaining and report the count. Primary endpoint macro Recall@40. Keep BM25 body, body/address field RRF, direct identifiers and uniform graph votes as locked above. Add two degree policies (a) hard gate: suppress outgoing allocation by every anchor whose observed number of distinct non-anchor neighbors exceeds the 40-function output budget; (b) mass-preserving allocation: divide that anchor's graph contribution by the number of its distinct non-anchor neighbors before applying the maximum across anchors. Thus one observation cannot spend more than half of its direct bonus in total before the max aggregation. The latter is the designated primary challenger versus uniform graph. Primary and supplementary metrics as pool protocol, with paired issue-level bootstrap stratified by repo. New graph policy sizes are tied to the actual retrieval budget. No optimization over thresholds. Persist per-method top-40 candidate IDs/scores/supporter degrees, seeds, hashes and all outcomes including zeros. Small holdout is exploratory.
