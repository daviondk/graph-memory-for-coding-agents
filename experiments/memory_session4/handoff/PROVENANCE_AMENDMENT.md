No official per-issue success/failure labels or Qwen decisions were inspected during this provenance audit. At collection, every submitted patch was fetched from the evaluator's patch.diff. Its bytes after UTF-8 decode were compared to info.submission from the same issue's linked original trajectory. Of 500 matched author links for one submission (GPT-5.2-Codex), only 41 are byte-equal and 459 differ. One structural check on astropy-12907, outside the registered reader sample, has different change text, beyond a newline/format difference. Claude 492/500 equal, others have empty evaluator patch and no non-empty final submission string. Gemini: all 441 exact. These observations establish artifact mismatch, not its cause.
