# Opportunity to observe a label under a constrained budget
Added before projection or inspection of any empirical associations. In addition to scoring all predictions against the truly observed second attempt, perform a simulated fixed-capacity retry queue. After common full-feedback warmup on the first half by decision time, take blocks of 100 consecutive candidates (each has its first failure visible) and allocate 20% of each block; tolerate a partial final block. Snapshot the history at the earliest candidate's first finish; later members' observable symptoms are assumed present when jointly prioritised, but never use outcomes from within the block to rank it. Only later store retry outcomes of actually chosen candidates when available; log all choices. Compare exactly matched quota and history acquisition against a global base, state-only, project-only, repo+status, candidate, last matching outcome, random-by-hash. Also simulate a teacher-forced diagnostic with exactly the same budget decisions and all previous observed outcomes available for updates; compare to selective-feedback. Optional 1/5 of capacity for hash-selected exploration per block, fixed before scoring, to detect self-reinforcing lookup history; exploratory. Main criterion number of genuine recoveries captured and measured seconds of selected historical retries; retain Brier and strong baselines as registered. This is a replay of a batched decision problem with measured outcome costs, not an intervention in real developer scheduling. Counterfactual assumes selecting a retry does not change another run's potential result, and reruns not attempted by historical developers have unknown outcome.
